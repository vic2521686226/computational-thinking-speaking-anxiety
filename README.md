# Under the Gaze — Speaking Anxiety Study

A self-contained HTML, CSS, and JavaScript prototype exploring classroom speaking anxiety through two perceived sources: external evaluation and self-judgment. Simulated heartbeat and breathing rates respond to anxiety levels and can be adjusted with the arrow keys.

## Files

- `index.html` contains the page structure and controls.
- `styles.css` contains the visual design and responsive layout.
- `script.js` updates the eye sizes, simulated rates, and heart animation.

Keep all three files together in the same folder.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No build step, dependencies, or external services are required.

## Interaction

- Adjust **External evaluation** and **Self-judgment** with their sliders. Each eye grows with its anxiety level, and they move closer together as the combined anxiety rises.
- The two anxiety levels automatically affect the simulated heartbeat and breathing rates.
- Use **↑ / ↓** to adjust the simulated heartbeat and **← / →** to adjust simulated breathing. Hold **Shift** for larger steps.
- Heart movement represents heartbeat; its expansion and contraction represent breathing.

The rates are illustrative interaction values, not medical measurements or a diagnosis.
